Rules

Status is a grey dot plus words, never a filled pastel pill. Pills are reserved for ratings and flags.
Money: currency code, thousands separators, two decimals in tables, none in hero figures; always right-aligned and tabular.
One primary button per view. The accent appears nowhere else except active navigation and links.
Borders separate; shadows only on things that float (popovers, menus).
No internal identifiers, codes or configuration words in any user-facing text. Proof and hashes live behind "View public proof".
Every list has a skeleton that matches its final layout, an empty state with a sample row, and an inline error with one recovery action.
